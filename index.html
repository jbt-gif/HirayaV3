About Hiraya

We're a small team that builds big systems.

Hiraya Systems is a technical AI agency founded in 2024. We're a small team — which means when you work with us, you work directly with the people building your systems, not an account manager routing requests.

We build with agentic AI — systems that reason, take independent actions, and operate continuously across your business. Our tools don't just answer questions. They manage pipelines, monitor operations, and surface insights without being asked.

Hiraya — an ancient word for the fruit of one's hopes and aspirations. That's exactly what automation is: infrastructure that makes your real goals achievable.
