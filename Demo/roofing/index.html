Common Questions

Questions homeowners always ask.

Straight answers to what we hear most. Don't see yours? Ask Scout below or book a free inspection.

How much does a full roof replacement cost?
Houston shingle replacements typically run $8,000–$15,000 for 2,000–2,500 sq ft homes. Metal runs $15,000–$25,000 and lasts 40–70 years. Tile runs $20,000–$30,000. Final price depends on slope, decking condition, and material choice — we give a written line-item quote after a free inspection.
Will my insurance cover storm or hail damage?
Most Texas homeowner policies cover storm and hail damage if filed within the deadline. We document the damage with photos, write the scope, and work directly with your adjuster — we've handled claims with every major carrier in Texas. You pay your deductible; we handle the rest.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most Houston homes (2,000–2,500 sq ft) take 1–2 days from tear-off to cleanup. Complex roofs or weather delays can push it to 3 days. We start early, work efficiently, and do magnetic nail sweeps every evening so your yard stays safe.
What does your 25-year warranty cover?
Two warranties in writing: a 25-year workmanship warranty from us (covers any install defect we caused) plus the manufacturer's material warranty (typically 30–50 years on premium shingles). Both transfer if you sell the home. One call, one of us picks up.
Are inspections really free?
Yes — no charge, no obligation. We climb up, take photos, document everything, and email you a report. If you don't need a roof, we'll tell you. We'd rather earn trust now than chase a sale we haven't deserved.
What materials do you use?
GAF and Owens Corning architectural shingles for most jobs (they hold up in Houston heat and storms). Standing-seam metal from premium TX suppliers when going metal. Synthetic underlayment, ice-and-water shield at valleys, and proper ridge vent — never the cheap stuff.
Are you licensed and insured in Texas?
Fully licensed (TX License #ROO-024517), bonded, and carry $2M general liability plus workers' comp on every crew member. Certificates available on request — never hire a roofer who can't show you both.
How fast can you get out for an inspection?
Same-week, usually within 2–3 business days. If you just had a storm or active leak, we'll prioritize you — call or book online and we'll work you in as fast as we can.
